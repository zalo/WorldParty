World Party: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy describes what World Party ("the game", "we") collects when you play at worldparty.makeshifted.workers.dev or as a Discord Activity, and what happens to it. The short version: the game keeps the shared world of each room, shows your name to the other players while you are connected, and stores nothing else about you.

What we collect

  • Discord account details. When you launch the game as a Discord Activity and allow it to identify you (Discord's identify permission), Discord tells the game your user id, username, display name and avatar. The game uses your display name (or username) as your player name so other players can see who they are playing with. The name is held in the room's memory only while you are connected and is not written to storage. The Discord access token is exchanged on our server and returned to your own client; the server does not keep it. No other Discord data is requested, and nothing is posted to Discord on your behalf.
  • Gameplay data. While you play, your client sends the room your position, your actions (firing, digging, building, votes) and chat messages. Positions and actions are relayed to the other players in the room and used to run the match. Chat messages are relayed to the players present and are not stored. The shared world itself (terrain, built structures, props, supplies and the match state) is stored per room so the room survives restarts; it is not linked to any account.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes the connection (IP address, browser type, request logs) to deliver the service and protect it from abuse, and keeps short-lived operational logs. We use these logs only to debug the service.

The game does not use cookies, advertising or analytics trackers, and does not sell or share personal data with anyone.

Where it is kept

Room data is stored in Cloudflare Durable Objects. Rooms may be reset or deleted at any time, by the players (a vote) or by us. Player names and connection details are discarded when you disconnect.

Your choices

  • You can decline the Discord sign-in: the game still works and you appear under a generic name such as "Player 3".
  • You can revoke the game's access to your Discord account at any time under Discord's User Settings, Authorized Apps.
  • To have a room's stored world deleted, contact us with the room name.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Discord's own age requirements apply inside Discord.

Changes
